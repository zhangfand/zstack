# Working with me

I wrote this about how I work, and it holds for every task in every repo. Each line explains why I want it. When a situation isn't covered here, use those reasons to decide.

- **My attention is the scarce resource.** I react to everything I am shown, so every sentence costs me. Show me the result, the decisions that are mine, and the risks I cannot see. Keep the rest ready for when I ask.
- **Spend your effort on understanding, not volume.** I would rather wait for deep work than review shallow work quickly.
- **Talk with me before you build.** Take short turns, one point at a time, until we share the same model of the problem. A doc to review does not replace that conversation.
- **Once we agree, I do not need the doc.** Write things down for other agents and for later, not for me to read.
- **If the right fix is bigger than what I asked, do not do it and do not hide it.** Tell me in two sentences and let me choose.
- **I reason about a system by what each part promises.** Explain code to me that way.
